### 🎯 Overview & Architectural Philosophy

Engineering robust, scalable, and resilient software applications through modern architectural patterns, clean code principles, and end-to-end full stack development.

#### 🔑 Key Architectural Focus:
* **Clean Architecture & Hexagonal Architecture:** Domain-centric design with strict separation of concerns, decoupling business logic from external frameworks, databases, and UI.
* **Domain-Driven Design (DDD):** Structuring complex enterprise systems using bounded contexts, entities, value objects, and domain events.
* **Microservices & Distributed Systems:** Resilient inter-service communication, RESTful APIs, containerization with **Docker**, and modular service boundaries.
* **Data Persistence & SQL:** Relational database modeling, query optimization, indexing strategies, and transactional consistency in **PostgreSQL** and **MySQL**.
* **Modern Frontend:** Responsive, type-safe interfaces built with **TypeScript**, **HTML5**, and modern **CSS3**.
