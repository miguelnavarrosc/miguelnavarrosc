### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer** specializing in **Automation & Systems Integration**, **Full Stack Architecture**, **AI Engineering**, and **Frontier Scientific Computing**.

- ⚙️ **Automation & Systems Integration:** Architecting enterprise automation solutions, RPA bots, and resilient API pipelines connecting platforms like **Microsoft 365 (Graph API)**, **Oracle CX Sales**, **Oracle Eloqua**, **Meta Ads**, and **Canvas LMS**.
- 🏛️ **Architecture & Full Stack:** Building scalable, maintainable distributed systems applying **Clean Architecture**, **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**, **Microservices**, and relational database optimization (**PostgreSQL**, **MySQL**).
- 🧠 **AI & Generative Systems:** Developing intelligent solutions leveraging **Generative AI**, **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**, and Machine Learning pipelines.
- 🔬 **Research & Frontier Lab:** Active researcher exploring **academic thesis work**, **advanced algorithms & computational complexity**, theoretical foundations of **Generative AI**, and **quantum computing (Qiskit)**.
